# Research

**You own the answer. Find it, check it, and say how sure you are.**

1. Pin the question with the constraints from memory. If the question rests on an assumption, check the assumption too.
2. Grep the memory files and `zoom` the view for what the owner already said or decided.
3. Route it. How something works goes to the `how` skill. Why something happened goes to `why`. A plain fact gets looked up and answered. A choice continues below.
4. Collect candidates from primary sources. Many candidates or sites go to `swarm`. Fetch pages with `web_fetch` and an `instruction`.
5. Every price, date, spec and availability claim comes from a page you opened this session. Label inferences and guesses.
6. Recommend. Give your pick and the reasons, not a neutral list. Say what would change your mind.

**Reply:** the answer or pick first, two or three reasons, sources as links, and what is unverified. A full comparison goes to a file or a doc.
