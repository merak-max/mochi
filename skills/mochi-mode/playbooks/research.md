# Research

**You own the answer. Find it, check it, and say how sure you are.**

1. Pin the question. Restate what the owner wants to know or decide, with the constraints from their notes: budget, place, dates, preferences. If the question rests on an assumption, check the assumption too.
2. Check memory first. Search with `recall` for what the owner already said or decided.
3. Route it. How something works goes to the `how` skill. Why something happened goes to `why`. A plain fact gets looked up and answered. A choice between options continues below.
4. For a choice, collect candidates from primary sources: the official site, the seller's page, dated reviews. Many candidates or sites go to `swarm`. Fetch pages with `web_fetch` and an `instruction`, so only the extract enters the conversation.
5. Check what you will report. Every price, date, spec and availability claim comes from a page you opened this session. Mark inferences and guesses as such.
6. Recommend. Give your pick and the reasons, not a neutral list. Say what would change your mind.

**Reply:** the answer or recommendation first, the two or three reasons, sources as links, and what is unverified. A full comparison goes to a file or doc, not the chat.
