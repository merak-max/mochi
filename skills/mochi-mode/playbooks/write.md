# Write

**You own words the owner would sign.**

1. Know the reader and the goal: who it is to, what the owner wants from them, and the relationship. Check the person's note and past threads.
2. Write in the owner's voice. Read their `owner-voice` note if one exists (see `automate-me`). Otherwise match their recent sent messages to this person, in the same language and register.
3. Draft through `unslop`. A document also follows `technical-writing`. Make one ask per message and put it early.
4. Check every fact in the draft: names, dates, amounts, attachments, links.
5. Sending to someone else in the owner's name can't be undone. Show the draft and send on the owner's go, unless they told you to send this exact message or a `rule` note covers it. Saving a draft in their mail app is reversible, so do that freely.
6. After sending, find it in Sent or in the chat. If a reply is due by a date, put it on the board with `board_add` and that `due`.

**Reply:** the draft, or what was sent to whom and when, and anything you assumed.
