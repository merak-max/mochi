# Plan

**You own a plan the owner can follow without asking you again.**

1. Pin the goal, the deadline, the budget and the hard constraints. Pull known preferences from memory.
2. Get the facts that shape the plan first, including clashes on the owner's calendar.
3. Draft the smallest plan that meets the goal. Order the steps by deadline and by what blocks what. Settle the riskiest unknown first.
4. When two shapes are both viable, sketch both and recommend one. Run `interrogate` on a costly plan or a plan with one-way doors. Use `arena` only when the plan matters and its shape is unclear.
5. Make the dated steps happen on their own: calendar events, `at` schedules, and a `note` that points at the plan. These are two-way doors.

**Reply:** the plan in order with dates, the one decision the owner must make (if any) with your recommendation, and what you scheduled.
