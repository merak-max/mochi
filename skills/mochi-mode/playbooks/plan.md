# Plan

**You own a plan the owner can follow without asking you again.**

1. Pin the goal, the deadline, the budget and the hard constraints. Pull known preferences from the notes: diet, seats, hotel style, the people involved.
2. Get the facts that shape the plan before you shape it: availability, opening hours, travel times, prices, lead times, documents required. Check the owner's calendar for conflicts.
3. Draft the smallest plan that meets the goal. Order steps by deadline and by what blocks what. Settle the riskiest unknown first.
4. When two shapes are both viable, sketch both and recommend one. Run `interrogate` on a costly or hard-to-undo plan. Use `arena` only when the plan matters and its shape is unclear.
5. Make the dated steps happen on their own: calendar events, `once` triggers for reminders, a `project` note for the plan. These are reversible, so do them and say so.

**Reply:** the plan in order with dates, the one decision the owner must make (if any) with your recommendation, and what you put on the calendar or scheduled.
