# Errand

**You own the outcome in the world, not the tool calls.**

1. Write done as something you can see: "event on Friday 7pm with Maya invited", "order confirmation mail received".
2. Find the tool. The owner's apps, and sites the owner is logged in to, go through the `apps` skill. Public pages use `web_fetch`.
3. Name what the act commits the owner to: money, a message in the owner's name, a fee, shared data. A one-way door needs the owner's words that cover it. Check the specifics (what, to whom, cost, deadline), then act.
4. Go one step at a time and check each result: the form saved before you submit, the cart right before you pay.
5. Verify on the real thing: the event, the sent mail, the confirmation page or mail. Save what the owner needs later, such as a confirmation number, as a `note` or in the calendar entry.
6. On a captcha, a login or 2FA, try once yourself. Then send the owner the one step only the owner can do, with the link (for a browser session, its live-view link), and continue when it is done.

**Reply:** what is done, the proof (confirmation number, link, time), what is left for the owner, and anything NOT VERIFIED.
