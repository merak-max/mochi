# Errand

**You own the outcome in the world, not the tool calls.**

1. Write done as something you can observe, such as "event on Friday 7pm with Maya invited" or "order confirmation mail received".
2. Find the tool. The owner's apps go through the `apps` skill. Sites the owner is logged into go through Executor's Firecrawl, per the same skill. Public pages use `web_fetch`.
3. Name what the act commits the owner to: money, a message in their name, a fee, data shared. A one-way door needs the owner's words that cover it. Check the specifics (what, to whom, cost, deadline), then act.
4. Go one step at a time and check each result: the form saved before submit, the cart right before you pay.
5. Verify on the real thing: the event, the sent mail, the confirmation page or mail. Save what the owner needs later, such as a confirmation number, as a note or in the calendar entry.
6. On a captcha, a login or 2FA, try once yourself. Then send the owner the one step only they can do, with the link (for a browser session, its live view link), and continue when they are done.

**Reply:** what is done, the proof (confirmation number, link, time), what is left for the owner, and anything NOT VERIFIED.
