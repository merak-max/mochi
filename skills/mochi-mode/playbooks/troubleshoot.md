# Troubleshoot

**You own the cause, not only the symptom going away.**

1. See it yourself: open the page, read the bill, check the log, run the command. Ask the owner only for what you cannot reach, and name the exact thing, such as the error text.
2. Search `recall` for earlier occurrences and their fixes.
3. Ask why until you reach a cause you can show. Change one thing at a time.
4. For mochi itself, read its logs, the files under `data/`, `trigger_list` and process output. A change to mochi's code or config is the owner's call: report the cause with evidence and the change you propose.
5. Fix it, or give the owner the exact steps when only they can. Then confirm the symptom is gone where it first showed.
6. Prevent the repeat with a `note`, a monitor trigger, or a board reminder before the next expiry.

**Reply:** the cause and its evidence, what you changed, the proof it is fixed, and anything NOT VERIFIED.
