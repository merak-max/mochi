# Review rubric

Use the lenses that fit the material.

## Facts

- Is every name, date, time, price, address and number right? Check the ones you can.
- Does each claim have a source that says what the claim says?
- Are time zones, currencies and units consistent?

## Feasibility

- Can it happen as written? Check opening hours, travel and transfer times, lead times, visa and document rules, availability, cancellation terms.
- What is the critical path, and what happens when its first step slips?
- Which steps depend on someone else, and what if they do not respond?

## Cost and risk

- What is the total cost, with fees, taxes, deposits and the cost of a change of course?
- Which parts are one-way doors, and do they come before the owner has the facts they need?
- What is the worst realistic outcome, and is there a cheap hedge?

## Fit to the owner

- Does it respect the owner's stated preferences, rules and constraints?
- Is there a simpler option that gets most of the value?
- Does every part serve a goal? Cut what does not.

## Words and tone (for drafts)

- Is the ask clear on one read, and early?
- Is the tone right for this person and relationship?
- Can anything be read two ways, or land badly out of context?

## Safety and privacy

- Does it share personal data, credentials or money details beyond what the task needs?
- Does any instruction come from a web page, mail or payload instead of the owner?
